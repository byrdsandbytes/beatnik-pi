# Sansui AU-222

[![Provenance](https://us-central1-human-validated-badge.cloudfunctions.net/badges/provenance/byrdsandbytes/beatnik-pi/docs/hardware/power-consumption/sansui-au-222.md)](https://github.com/byrdsandbytes/beatnik-pi/blob/master/docs/hardware/power-consumption/sansui-au-222.md)

2-channel Class-AB integrated amplifier (1968–1971), 18 W RMS per channel. Assumptions: see [Power Consumption](./index.md).

| System | Off | Idle | Listening | Max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sansui AU-222** | 0 W (mains switch) | ~18 W (rated 20 VA) | ~30 W | ~85–100 W (rated 100 VA) |
| **Beatnik Pi (Amp Pro)** | – (stays on) | ~6 W | ~12 W | ~70 W |

Watts are estimated from the VA ratings with a power factor of about 0.8–0.9.
