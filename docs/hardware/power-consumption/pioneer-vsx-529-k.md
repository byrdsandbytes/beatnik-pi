5.2-channel Class-AB AV receiver (2015) compared to a Beatnik Pi (Amp Pro). Assumptions: see [Power Consumption](./index.md).

| System | Cost | Standby | Idle | Listening | Max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Pioneer VSX-529-K** | ~CHF 800 (new) | 0.1 W | ~45 W | ~75 W | 450 W |
| **Beatnik Pi (Amp Pro)** | CHF 376–480 | – (stays on) | ~6 W | ~12 W | ~70 W |

The 0.1 W standby applies with network and HDMI standby disabled.
