# Example: Pioneer VSX-529-K

[![Provenance](https://us-central1-human-validated-badge.cloudfunctions.net/badges/provenance/byrdsandbytes/beatnik-pi/docs/hardware/power-consumption/pioneer-vsx-529-k.md)](https://github.com/byrdsandbytes/beatnik-pi/blob/master/docs/hardware/power-consumption/pioneer-vsx-529-k.md)

A typical modern AV receiver compared to a Beatnik Pi (Amp Pro). For the general assumptions see [Power Consumption](./index.md).

::: info Short version
Buying a Beatnik Pi instead of this receiver saves **CHF 400 to 1,350 over five years**, purchase price included. If you already own the receiver and leave it on, a Beatnik Pi saves **CHF 115 to 190 per year** in electricity.
:::

## Power Draw

| System | Notes | Cost | Standby / Off | Idle (On) | Typical Listening | Max Rated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Pioneer VSX-529-K** | 5.2-ch Class-AB AV receiver (mfg. 2015), HDMI video processing, high waste heat | ~CHF 800 (new) | 0.1 W *(higher with HDMI or network standby)* | ~45 W *(40–55 W)* | ~75 W *(60–100 W)* | 450 W |
| **Beatnik Pi (Amp Pro)** | Class-D, see [Power Consumption](./index.md) | CHF 376–480 | – *(stays on)* | ~6 W | ~12 W | ~70 W |

The 0.1 W standby only applies with network and HDMI standby disabled. With those enabled (needed for app control or HDMI-CEC), standby draw is higher and the savings grow.

If you replace a receiver that still works, the electricity savings pay back the Beatnik Pi in **2–2.6 years** with continuous playback, and in **3–4 years** if the receiver would otherwise be left on.

With several rooms the savings add up: two Beatnik Pis replacing two receivers that are left on save around **CHF 280 per year**.
