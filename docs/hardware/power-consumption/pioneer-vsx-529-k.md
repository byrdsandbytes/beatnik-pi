# Pioneer VSX-529-K

[![Provenance](https://us-central1-human-validated-badge.cloudfunctions.net/badges/provenance/byrdsandbytes/beatnik-pi/docs/hardware/power-consumption/pioneer-vsx-529-k.md)](https://github.com/byrdsandbytes/beatnik-pi/blob/master/docs/hardware/power-consumption/pioneer-vsx-529-k.md)

5.2-channel Class-AB AV receiver (2015) with HDMI video processing. Assumptions: see [Power Consumption](./index.md).

| System | Standby | Idle | Listening | Max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Pioneer VSX-529-K** | 0.1 W | ~45 W | ~75 W | 450 W |
| **Beatnik Pi (Amp Pro)** | – (stays on) | ~6 W | ~12 W | ~70 W |

The 0.1 W standby applies with network and HDMI standby disabled.
